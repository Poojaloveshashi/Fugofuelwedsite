Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Fugo Fuel ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website www.fugofuel.com and the Fugo Fuel mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our onsite vehicle repair, roadside assistance, battery jumpstart/replacement, and mobile EV rescue services in Hyderabad, India.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, phone number (for OTP authentication and technician coordination), and email address.
  • Precise Location Data (GPS): Real-time geographic location collected when you request roadside assistance, onsite repairs, battery delivery, or mobile EV rescue so we can dispatch the nearest technician or partner workshop.
  • Vehicle & Order Details: Vehicle make, model, registration number, service history, and billing/GST invoice records.
  • Partner Workshop Data: For garage and warehouse partners, we collect business name, workshop address, technician details, and battery/parts catalog listings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To dispatch mobile mechanics, battery technicians, and EV rescue units to your location within our target SLA.
  • To connect customers with verified partner repair workshops and spare-parts warehouses in Hyderabad.
  • To send booking confirmations, live technician tracking updates, OTP verifications, and digital receipts via SMS, WhatsApp, or in-app notifications.
  • To improve platform safety, prevent fraud, and maintain service quality.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share necessary order details (name, phone number, vehicle info, and drop-pin location) strictly with:

  • Assigned Fugo Fuel technicians and verified partner service warehouses fulfilling your active service request.
  • Payment gateway providers (for UPI/card processing) and cloud infrastructure providers required to operate the app securely.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your service records as required for warranty tracking and Indian tax/GST compliance. You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing us at support@fugofuel.com.

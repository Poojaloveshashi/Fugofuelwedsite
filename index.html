FAQ

Good to know.

Quick answers before you order.

How fast will Fugo reach me?

We aim to be with you in about 15 minutes. Actual arrival depends on your location and traffic.

Which fuels can I order?

Petrol and diesel. Tell us which one your vehicle needs when you place the order.

What repairs can you handle on the spot?

Quick fixes such as flat tyres, dead batteries and minor faults. If a job needs a workshop, we'll tell you upfront.

Is it safe to have fuel delivered?

Safety comes first. Fuel is handled by trained staff, with proper containers and equipment, and only with your vehicle switched off.

Where is Fugo available?

We're launching area by area. Share your location when you order and we'll confirm whether we can reach you.

Can businesses and fleets use Fugo?

Yes. Fugo works for fleets, delivery vehicles and businesses. Get in touch and we'll set it up around your routes.
